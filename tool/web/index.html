ScopeCard audit

Compare a benchmark release with the universe it claims to cover. The scope card reports what the release can never evaluate (W), or only what it covers when its sampling rule is not shown, whether a simple rule reproduces it, and how large a difference on the excluded part would have to be to reverse an AUC comparison. No labels, models or GPU are needed.

What the card's terms mean
W
The share of the universe that the benchmark can never evaluate, counted in the units the card names (entities, user–item pairs, topics). When the release does not show how its pairs were drawn, the card reports the share it does not cover instead.
W_pairs
The same share counted over candidate pairs (users × items, or pairs of units) rather than single units.
Jaccard
How well a simple construction rule reproduces the release: the overlap between the units the rule keeps and the units the release contains (1 = exactly; 0.95 or more = approximately).
d_min
The smallest depth threshold (facts, references or interactions per unit) at which the benchmark covers at least half of the restricted universe (W ≤ 0.5): the narrowest population it represents well.
c*
For a difference ΔO between two models on the benchmark, in AUC: how large the change between the benchmark and the excluded part (c = ΔO − ΔU) must be, with the sign of ΔO, for the ordering to reverse on the universe. It needs no labels; c itself does.
