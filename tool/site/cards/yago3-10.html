YAGO3-10

Comparisons on YAGO3-10 license claims about the entities it can evaluate, which leave out 95.4% of the 2,635,315 YAGO 3.0.2 entities that occur in at least one triple of its 37 relation types. The closest simple rule (both_ends, T = 10) is approximately reproduced (Jaccard 0.967).

From the 2026-10 pilot

Task
KG link prediction
Domain
knowledge-graph link prediction
Rule stated in the paper
'a subset of YAGO3 which consists of entities which have a minimum of 10 relations each' (YAGO3 version not stated); Rossi et al. read it as >= 10 different relations plus all facts of their neighbours
What the release shows
entities with >= 10 yagoFacts facts, keeping only facts whose BOTH ends qualify (one pass): 123189 entities (Jaccard 0.967 with the release), 1089132 triples; the release equals the 3.0.2 facts induced on its entity set (0 induced facts missing); the 'distinct relations' reading gives 546 seeds (Jaccard 0.004) and the 'neighbours' reading 2338580 entities (Jaccard 0.052)
Rule decidable from the paper
partial. rule reimplemented on YAGO 3.0.2 (Jaccard 0.967); not exact: 1054372 of 1089040 release triples are 3.0.2 facts and 2046 release entities have < 10 facts in 3.0.2, plausibly a version difference that the unstated version prevents checking
Negatives
implicit: every corrupted triple over the entity set, filtered of known triples
Evaluation
filtered ranking of head and tail against all 123182 entities; MRR, Hits@k
Sources checked
Dettmers et al. 2018 AAAI (ConvE, datasets section); Rossi et al. 2021 TKDD (Sec. 4.1); ConvE repo YAGO3-10.tar.gz; YAGO 3.0.2 yagoFacts.ttl (yago-knowledge.org/data/yago3/tmp)
Notes
inside the release 52437 entities have < 10 triples: the threshold applies in the source KG, not in the benchmark graph

Scope card: YAGO3-10

Comparisons on YAGO3-10 license claims about the entities it can evaluate, which leave out 95.4% of the 2,635,315 YAGO 3.0.2 entities that occur in at least one triple of its 37 relation types. The closest simple rule (both_ends, T = 10) is approximately reproduced (Jaccard 0.967).
  • Task: kg_link_prediction
  • Universe: YAGO 3.0.2 yagoFacts.ttl
  • W is a share of: the 2,635,315 YAGO 3.0.2 entities that occur in at least one triple of its 37 relation types
  • Definitions: Universe = entities carrying >= 1 universe triple of the release relation types. Depth = number of universe triples on the entity. Release entities absent from the universe count against every rule in the Jaccard.
  • Tool: benchscope-audit 0.0.1 (schema benchscope-card/0.3)
  • Universe fingerprint: sha256:8708a2b249492f1eef68d5d165768804efc3861b8c0178b5da78afcd9a648c2c
  • Candidates: every entity of the release, in filtered head and tail ranking (excluded units cannot be evaluated). Link prediction ranks each test query against the release entity set, so a universe entity absent from the release is never a candidate.
