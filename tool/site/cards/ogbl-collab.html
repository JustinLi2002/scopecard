Task

Domain
graph link prediction
Rule stated in the paper
'a subset of the collaboration network between authors indexed by MAG'; train <= 2017, valid 2018, test 2019
What the release shows
every one of the 235868 authors has a training (<= 2017) collaboration, and 0 valid/test positives touch an author without one; negatives are uniform over node pairs (lower-degree half share 0.502); 0.366 of test positives repeat a training pair
Rule decidable from the paper
undecidable. release only: the T = 1 restriction is a property of the release; whether it is the subset rule or a consequence of it cannot be told
Negatives
sampled: 100,000 random node pairs per split (Hits@50)
Evaluation
Hits@50 against 100,000 sampled negative pairs
Sources checked
Hu et al. 2020 NeurIPS (OGB, Sec. 5.2); ogb.stanford.edu linkprop page; collab.zip v1 (raw, split/time, mapping)
