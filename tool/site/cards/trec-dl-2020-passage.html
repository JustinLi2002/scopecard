TREC DL 2020 passage

Comparisons on TREC DL 2020 passage license claims about the queries it can evaluate, which leave out 73.0% of the 200 TREC DL 2020 passage test topics. Within each evaluated query, 99.9958% to 99.9983% of the 8,841,823 documents are unjudged and count as non-relevant. The stated rule (>= 3 relevant (grade >= 2) and relevant/judged < 0.6) is consistent with every released unit; excluded units cannot be checked (no published judgments).

The 2020 overview does not state the topic rule; the companion SIGIR paper (Craswell et al. 2021, Sec. 3.2) says the 2019 judgment process was reused, so the card checks the 2019 thresholds.
