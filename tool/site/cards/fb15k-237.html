FB15k-237

No card yet. Its universe, the 2013 Freebase dump, is no longer distributed. The pilot checked the relation rule and the pair rule against FB15k; those checks have no tool pipeline yet.

From the 2026-10 pilot

Task
KG link prediction
Domain
knowledge-graph link prediction
Rule stated in the paper
FB15k: Freebase entities present in Wikilinks with >= 100 Freebase mentions (entities and relations); FB15k-237: the 401 most frequent FB15k relations, near-duplicate/inverse relations (>= 97% pair overlap) reduced to one each (237), and valid/test triples dropped if their entity pair is directly linked in training
What the release shows
relation rule consistent: 401st relation on FB15k train has 100 triples (3 tied), every one of the 164 dropped relations has a kept partner with >= 97% overlap, but 28 kept pairs also overlap >= 97% of the smaller set (3 of the larger); pair rule exact: 0 valid/test triples linked in train, the rule removes 0.372 / 0.375 of FB15k valid/test triples on the 237 relations; NOT stated: 71 entities that still carry triples on the 237 relations were removed with all 4555 of their training triples
Rule decidable from the paper
partial. relation and test-pair rules checked against FB15k and the release (all 310116 FB15k-237 triples are FB15k triples); entity seed rule not checkable without a 2013 Freebase snapshot and Wikilinks
Negatives
implicit: every corrupted triple over the entity set, filtered of known triples
Evaluation
filtered ranking of head and tail against all 14541 entities; MRR, Hits@k
Sources checked
Bordes et al. 2013 NeurIPS (TransE, Sec. 4.1); Toutanova & Chen 2015 CVSC (Sec. 4.3); FB15k release (everest.hds.utc.fr fb15k.tgz via Wayback, README); FB15K-237.2.zip (Microsoft download, README); ConvE repo FB15k-237.tar.gz
Notes
the ConvE copy is triple-for-triple identical to the Microsoft release; the undocumented entity removal (one entity with 2867 triples) is the same class of silent change as [P3]'s MolTrans input blanking
