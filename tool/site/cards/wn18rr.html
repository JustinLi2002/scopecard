WN18RR

Comparisons on WN18RR license claims about the entities it can evaluate, which leave out 63.4% of the 112,199 WordNet 3.0 synsets that carry at least one triple of its 11 relations. The closest simple rule (both_ends, T = 5) is not reproduced (Jaccard 0.595).

From the 2026-10 pilot

Task
KG link prediction
Domain
knowledge-graph link prediction
Rule stated in the paper
'we filtered out the synsets appearing in less that 15 triplets' over the 18 relation types, giving 40,943 synsets (Mach Learn 2014); WN18RR keeps WN18's entities and drops inverse relations (11 relations)
What the release shows
WN18 is the induced subgraph of WordNet 3.0's 18-relation graph on its entity set (151437 of 151442 triples rebuilt; 58 induced triples absent), so the entity set is the rule; but 34393 of the 40943 synsets have < 15 triples in that graph and 38117 in the release itself; no degree threshold reproduces the set (best single-pass Jaccard 0.597, 15-core empty); entity ids are bare offsets, which WordNet reuses across POS: 161 WN18 entities merge triples of two different synsets
Rule decidable from the paper
partial. the rule cannot be reimplemented, but the release fixes the entity set exactly and WordNet 3.0 holds every excluded synset and triple (92998 of 93003 WN18RR triples rebuilt)
Negatives
implicit: every corrupted triple over the entity set, filtered of known triples
Evaluation
filtered ranking of head and tail against all 40943 entities; MRR, Hits@k; 210 of 3134 test triples have an entity absent from train
Sources checked
Bordes et al. 2014 Mach Learn 94:233 (Sec. 5.1, Table 2); Bordes et al. 2012 AISTATS; TransE 2013 (Sec. 4.1); Dettmers et al. 2018 AAAI (ConvE); wordnet-mlj12 release (README: 'extracted from WordNet 3.0'); ConvE repo WN18RR.tar.gz; Princeton WordNet-3.0.tar.gz
Notes

Scope card: WN18RR

Comparisons on WN18RR license claims about the entities it can evaluate, which leave out 63.4% of the 112,199 WordNet 3.0 synsets that carry at least one triple of its 11 relations. The closest simple rule (both_ends, T = 5) is not reproduced (Jaccard 0.595).
  • Task: kg_link_prediction
  • Universe: WordNet 3.0 pos_offset, 11 relations
  • W is a share of: the 112,199 WordNet 3.0 synsets that carry at least one triple of its 11 relations
  • Definitions: Universe = WordNet 3.0 synsets carrying >= 1 triple of the benchmark relations (W); W_all_synsets also counts synsets with none. Depth = number of universe triples on the synset.
  • Tool: scopecard 0.0.2 (schema scopecard/0.3)
  • Universe fingerprint: sha256:0e77d7b548e8f9dfa3db8792cbe0cb6d373395cda3e9e7bf51383f5049806f10
  • Candidates: every entity of the release, in filtered head and tail ranking (excluded units cannot be evaluated). Link prediction ranks each test query against the release entity set, so a universe entity absent from the release is never a candidate.
